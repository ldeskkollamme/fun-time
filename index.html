How to play

1

Connect. Enter your email and a 4-letter room code. Your rival enters the same code — when both are online, the duel starts automatically.

2

Hide a number. The picker taps one of the 5 circles. That number becomes the secret and the circles shuffle.

3

Find it. The finder has 12 seconds to tap the circle holding the secret number.

4

Score. Found it? The finder scores 1 point. Missed? Nobody scores.

5

Swap. Roles switch each round. The first player to 5 points wins.
